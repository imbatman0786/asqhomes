How We Work

A method refined
over a decade.

01
Discovery Call
We begin with a 60-minute conversation about your life, tastes, and how you use your space. No forms, no templates — just deep listening.
02
Concept Design
A comprehensive mood board, space plan, and material palette — refined through two rounds of feedback until every detail resonates.
03
Execution
We manage every vendor, contractor, and craftsman. Weekly updates, transparent budgeting, and zero surprises on handover day.
04
Final Styling
The finishing layer — art, accessories, textiles, plants, and light. Where a designed space becomes a truly lived-in home.
